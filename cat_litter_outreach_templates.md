# Cat Litter Outreach Templates — Bowling Green KY

Placeholders to fill: `[Your Name]`, `[Company]`, `[cat litter brand]`, `[Phone]`, `[Email]`, `[Shelter Name]`, `[Name]`, `[Practice/Facility]`.

Offer angles used: **bulk wholesale pricing**, **donated trial pallet/samples** (shelters only), **subscription / recurring delivery**.

---

## Phone script — S-Tier shelters / spay-neuter clinics

> Hi, this is **[Your Name]** with **[Company]** here in the Bowling Green area. I supply **[cat litter brand]**, and I work with local shelters three ways — **bulk wholesale pricing**, a **standing subscription** so you never run out, and I always keep some pallets set aside to **donate**. Shelter your size goes through serious litter — who's the best person to talk to, and would a **free sample pallet this week** help?

## Tips when using these

- **Open with the donation angle for shelters only.** It's the strongest hook for nonprofits and gets you in the door even when they're not ready to switch suppliers.
- **For vets/boarding, lead with subscription.** They hate reorder admin more than they care about per-unit price.
- **Best calling window:** shelters/clinics 10am–noon Tue/Thu (avoid Mon/Fri intake surge).
- **Always offer the sample first.** It costs you a pallet and gets a yes-meeting from people who'd otherwise stall.
